# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge
We are committed to making participation in this project a welcoming and respectful experience for everyone.

## Expected behaviour
- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent while addressing ideas critically.
- Accept feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the community.
- Respect different backgrounds and levels of experience.

## Unacceptable behaviour
Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, intimidation, and publishing private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Enforcement
Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions that violate this code of conduct. Serious or repeated violations may result in exclusion from project spaces.
